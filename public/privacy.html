2. What Waha actually does with your data

Waha is built so that most of what you enter never leaves your device. Concretely:

  • Stored only on your device (browser local storage), never sent to us: journal entries, mood log, exercise streak, calm plan, language and display preferences. We have no server-side database for this data — we cannot see it, back it up, or hand it over, because it never reaches us.
  • Sent to a third-party AI service to generate a reply: the text you type in the chat, mood check-in, or "rewrite my feelings" feature. This is sent to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI models) via our own server, which also stores it — again only on your device — so the chat can remember earlier turns.
  • Processed by our server but not stored by us: your IP address is used momentarily to apply a rate limit (so one visitor can't exhaust the app's AI budget) and is not logged alongside your messages.
  • Technical logs: our server may log whether a request succeeded or failed, and whether a reply was rewritten by our safety layer — never the content of your messages.

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

Chat messages, mood check-ins, and similar free-text entries can reveal information about your mental health, which GDPR treats as a special category of personal data (Art. 9). We rely on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), which you give before your first chat message is sent (the in-app consent step). You can withdraw consent at any time by not using the chat feature and deleting your local chat history via the in-app "Privacy Passport."

7. Your rights

You can, at any time, from the app's "Privacy Passport" screen:

  • Export all your locally-stored data as a file
  • Delete just your chat history, mood data, or exercise history
  • Delete everything at once

Under GDPR you additionally have the right to access, rectify, restrict, or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us at [privacy@yourdomain.com] for anything not covered by the in-app tools.
